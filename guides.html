THE BIG IDEA

Tracking is useful because it creates a record.

Memory is good at remembering unusually good or bad days. It is less reliable at describing an ordinary month. A tracker gives you a consistent record that you can inspect later.

The value comes from the decisions you make after seeing the pattern, not from collecting numbers for their own sake.

How to use these guides

Start with the beginner guide, then read the planning and review articles when your tracker has enough entries to show a pattern. Use the streak and 365-day articles when you need help interpreting longer runs of data.
