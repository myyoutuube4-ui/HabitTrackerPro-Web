FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Simple answers to the questions that matter when choosing a browser-based tracker.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core tracker. The web edition stores its main tracking data locally in your browser.

Can I use HabitTracker Pro on my phone?

Yes. The web interface is responsive and the tracker can be installed as a PWA where the browser supports installation.

Can I back up my tracking data?

Yes. Settings includes JSON export and restore tools. Browser folder backup is also available where supported by the browser.

What does the 365-day challenge measure?

The challenge uses the tracker’s daily score system. In the current web edition, a challenge day is counted when the overall daily score reaches 75% or higher.

Does the tracker require a cloud account?

No. The core tracking workflow is designed to work locally in the browser. You choose when to export or back up your data.

Will the tracker show ads?

The core tracker page is designed to stay focused. Public informational pages have reserved areas for advertising that can be enabled after the site owner completes the relevant advertising setup.
