Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how the HabitTracker Pro website and web tracker handle information. Review this page again before enabling any third-party analytics or advertising, and update it to match the services actually used.

01 · Tracker data02 · Backups03 · Notifications04 · Advertising05 · Analytics06 · Retention and deletion07 · Contact

1. Tracker data

The core web tracker stores habits, daily logs, notes, goals and preferences in browser storage on your device. No account is required for the core tracker. The site owner does not receive those local tracker entries simply because you use the tracker.

2. Backups

When you use Export Backup, a JSON file is generated in your browser for you to save. When you choose a folder for a browser-supported local backup, the browser may ask for permission to write to that folder. You control those browser permissions.

3. Notifications

Browser notifications are optional. Notification permission is requested by your browser and can be revoked through browser or device settings.

4. Advertising

Public informational pages may use Google AdSense or another advertising service after the site owner completes the relevant setup. Advertising technologies can use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to measure and personalize advertising according to the enabled configuration and applicable policies.

For users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google states that publishers using personalized ads must use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The site's final consent configuration must match the advertising setup that is actually enabled.

5. Analytics

No third-party analytics service is required for the core tracker. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to name the service, explain the relevant data use and provide the appropriate user controls before deployment.

6. Retention and deletion

Core tracker data remains in your browser storage until you change or clear it using browser controls or the tracker’s available settings. Exported backup files are controlled by you and remain wherever you choose to store them.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page and the published email address for the site owner.
