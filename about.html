THE PRODUCT

Small daily actions. Clearer long-term feedback.

The product grew from a practical question: what would a habit tracker look like if the daily action stayed compact while the historical feedback became richer?

The web edition brings together a daily page, 365-day matrix, calendar, analytics, goals, missions, XP, achievements, notes and reminders in one browser-based workflow.

H

Your history should help you understand your routine, not make the routine harder to manage.

HabitTracker Pro design principle

Our design principles

Clarity: the next action should be obvious. Visibility: progress should be easy to inspect. Control: your tracking data should be exportable. Consistency: the interface should support a repeatable daily workflow.

What the web edition stores

The core tracker stores habits, daily logs, notes, goals and preferences in browser storage. You can explicitly export a JSON backup, and additional browser-supported backup options are available from Settings.

Why we publish guides

The public guides are part of the product experience, not filler around advertisements. Each guide focuses on a practical habit-tracking question and is written to help readers use tracking data more effectively.

What HabitTracker Pro is not

It is a personal productivity and self-tracking tool, not a medical, financial, legal or mental-health service. Any goals or routines you create remain your own choices.

The information on this site is general educational content. It is not professional advice.
