# AdSense setup notes

This project contains placeholders only. Do not publish a fake publisher ID.

1. Replace `ca-pub-REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID` in `index.html` with the publisher ID from your AdSense account when Google gives you one.
2. Add the exact AdSense script Google provides inside `<head>` on the pages where it instructs you to place it.
3. Replace the placeholder `ads.txt` line with the exact line shown by AdSense for your publisher account.
4. Replace the placeholder domain in `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with your real HTTPS domain.
5. Replace the placeholder contact emails and review the privacy/terms pages before applying.
6. Place responsive ad units on content pages with real informational content rather than making the app itself an ad wall.

Google can review a site only after it is live, accessible and compliant; approval is not guaranteed by this template.

If you serve personalized Google ads to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google currently requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Choose the consent solution after creating your AdSense setup and verify its current requirements in AdSense.
